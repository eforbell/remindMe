Reminders

People

Each person needs their own brrr key to get reminders. Keys stay on this server and are never shown again in full.

Getting a brrr key

  1. Install brrr from the App Store on the person’s iPhone and allow notifications.
  2. In brrr, copy the webhook secret (it starts with br_usr_) or the whole webhook URL. Use the device’s own webhook to reach just that phone.
  3. Paste it here, save, and tap Send test.

For the Critical alert level, Critical Alerts must also be allowed for brrr in the phone’s Settings.
